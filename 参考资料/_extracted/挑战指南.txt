T5 Challenge IntroductionThis guide is not intended as a strict true way forward, but as a series of suggestions based on our experiences. Herein we outline  some 24 challenges (cblock) that can be completed with reasonable efficiency after defeating the fifth titan.The primary requirement for these challenges is 5000 levels in each of the challenge NGUs (cNGUs) that are readily accessible at this stage: Power, Augments, Windows and Gold. You may need a certain T5 drop to accomplish this. One of us chose to complete them at 8400 cNGUs instead, but the differences in times were minor. Note that you will benefit from running magic beards, i.e. reverse hitler + fu manchu + ladybeard[optional]. Remember that they only start growing at full magic cap.Another helpful ability is rich jerks’ attack/defense boosts in the experience tab. 1,000% in each is not too expensive and allows to push a boss further.Consider making a manual save file before starting challenges if your cNGUs are below 5000.Note that troll and no rebirth challenges are the only critical ones. Others can be delayed.T5 Challenge ListName	Abbreviation	Number of completions	Difficulty scales?
